❓ FAQ

What is Brain Mode?

A pattern where your AI agent delegates heavy work to subagents. The subagent's 50+ tool calls stay hidden from your context. You see 1 message per task instead of 200.

Do I need to install anything?

Yes — npx mem-context-setup installs both MCP servers, writes config, and creates the brain prompt. Takes 30 seconds.

Is this open source?

Yes. Both mem-context and mem-evolved are MIT licensed on GitHub. The paid products are convenience bundles — pre-built configs, prompts, and guides.
